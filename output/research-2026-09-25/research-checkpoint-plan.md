## Evidence database rules

Each record identifies its primary source, authors/institution where verified, task/language, actual evidence, reusable artifact, limitations, relevance, access/license uncertainty and check date. Distinguish author-reported experiments, direct static source inspection, independent replication, and untested Pahlavi transfer. Do not equate a public repository with runnable reproduction or a model-judge score with philological validation.

Follow central citations into original research. For named researchers use public professional information only; no contact is authorized. Record unresolved access, licensing and source discrepancies instead of inferring them. University affiliation supports provenance, not correctness by itself.

The endpoint is a traceable research base and a defensible proposed method with resource implications. It is not an assertion that all possible literature has been exhausted or that the selected approach has already passed Pahlavi evaluation.
