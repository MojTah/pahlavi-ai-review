## Completion evidence required before training design is finalized

1. **Source coverage:** consolidate the existing source manifests, research inventories and actual access evidence. Cover current Parsig data, the277 unadmitted translated-field records, the separate247 quarantined records, supplied grammar/reader/dictionary books, MacKenzie's Concise Pahlavi Dictionary, and relevant institutional collections. Record included, unsuitable, duplicate, held-out, or unresolved for each. New acquisition is targeted to credible missing coverage rather than an unlimited literature search. The known book/access gaps remain visible until resolved or explicitly accepted as limits.
2. **Attribution and meaning:** preserve source form, actual target language, sense distinctions, uncertainty, author/editor/translator, edition and locator. Separate published lexicon entries, pedagogical examples, attested whole-clause translations and new project annotations. An English gloss does not automatically become a Persian gold target. An example generated from a dictionary is synthetic and needs its own independent qualification. Restoring a missing citation does not bypass downstream language, alignment or meaning checks.
3. **Novelty and split protection:** retain the frozen benchmark and all16 DEV/TEST work exclusions, including alternate editions and answer-equivalent witnesses. Apply existing exact/near-copy checks plus work/lineage review. Exclude held-out example sentences embedded in dictionaries/grammars; generic lexical entries and contextual quotations require separate treatment. Repeated spellings, inflections and alternate targets are not independent new attestations. Never turn test answers or reviewer corrections into training labels.
4. **Coverage and balance:** report unique qualified parent contexts, works and lexical senses; count source/target tokens as well as rows. Show what each addition contributes to known-word translation, contextual composition and uncertainty handling. Do not flood whole-translation training with thousands of short gloss targets or overcount derivative variants. Report unresolved gaps instead of manufacturing examples to fill them.
5. **Independent readiness review:** verify extraction against source pages/entries, review alignment and linguistic qualifications at their actual evidence strength, preserve disagreements/quarantine, and produce one consolidated readiness report. AI review remains provisional; specialist certification must not be claimed. The staged seven S22 pairs have image verification but are not admitted by that fact alone.
6. **Freeze the resulting data version:** save lineage, exclusions, task types, uncertainty policy and checksums, with a complete input/target census under the proposed tokenizer/template. The existing TRAIN2237 and fixed evaluation remain immutable historical controls. No newly discovered source is appended during a paid run.
7. **Then choose one justified training comparison:** use the resulting data size, task mixture and remaining budget to settle backbone, full/parameter-efficient adaptation, objective, sampling/exposure and stopping rule. Retain qualified Gemma as the current quality reference; no automatic commitment to Gemma, NLLB or another model is made before the data evidence. Compare using the unchanged separate-rater semantic merit, preserve every failure, and require the existing promotion and PAL-REF checks before deployment. Fresh funding/rate/idle-job/readiness checks remain mandatory, under the cumulativeUSD25 cap.
