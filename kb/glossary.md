Do not infer a heterogram from a semantic gloss. Do not label a modern Persian cognate as a Middle Persian attestation. The Frahang contains transmitted, corrupt, and otherwise unattested entries; an entry there needs contextual checking before reuse. [MacKenzie on the Frahang](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/frahang-i-pahlawig/)

**Gētīg/mēnōg** must not be reduced to good/evil, human/divine, or a universal modern physical/psychological binary. Their theological relationship depends on the passage. [Shaked, discussion of complementarity and late usage](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/getig-and-menog/).
