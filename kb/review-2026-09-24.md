# Pahlavi study review — 24 September 2026

Version **0.8.0**. The project studies **Middle Persian (Pahlavi)** for later translation into Farsi and English. It is historically related to Old Persian and modern Farsi, but is a distinct language stage.

## What is now complete

**Paragraph-layer reading of the saved Parsig snapshot is complete:** 126 records, 334 chapters and 4,507 unique numbered units from the 20 September collection. The earlier baseline was seven records / 52 units; this review added eight short records / 81 units, then all remaining 111 records / 4,374 units through seven readers. Every saved transcription field, Farsi field, additional translation/commentary layer and supplied note was included. Headings, colophons, quotations, fragments and editorial-only fields are included in these counts.

The [exact coverage ledger](../sources/parsig-complete-study-2026-09-24.json) records the source hash and reader assignments. The [inventory](../sources/parsig-live-inventory.json) records each work's chapter coverage. These verify the scope of the reading reports; hashes and ID coverage do not prove linguistic comprehension or translation accuracy. There is no claim to have read every manuscript, all word-level annotations, every introduction, every external archive, or all linked publications.

## What improved in the knowledge base

The grammar now explains omitted auxiliaries, finite versus non-finite ambiguity, overlapping person/mood endings, clitic resumption, past-stem alignment and attested late object-marking uses of rāy. It retains scholarly disagreement about ō rather than imposing a universal rule. The source example Zādspram 8.1 shows why plural agreement need not identify plural agents.

The glossary now gives narrower contextual meanings and distinguishes an attested dārīg from a restored occurrence. The script notes distinguish language from script, legacy-font strings from Unicode transcription, and editorial restoration from a translator's addition. No automatic font conversion or manuscript-reading competence is inferred.

## Findings that matter for translation

| Evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Parsig 110000007/010/021 and 124000003 contain attribution-only Farsi fields | The raw 4,445 populated-field count is not a usable translation-pair count. |
| 225001001 and 225002001 contain Persian editorial notes in the transcription field | A field name is not a language label or proof of primary text. |
| 134009001 has 1,800 in transcription/Farsi and 1,600 in English; 138005007 has a possible Farsi polarity mismatch | Preserve the exact editions and flag the difference; do not silently overwrite a published layer. |
| Cross-unit translations, duplicated passages and mixed Middle Persian/Parthian or quoted Avestan occur throughout the reader reports | Same record/row position does not establish sentence alignment or a monolingual corpus. |
| The Xusrō edition contains a Pahlavi rendering based on Arabic and a Persian version mediated through Arabic | Distinguish transmitted ancient text, secondary reconstruction and later version before alignment. |
| OpenAMPD MP0424/MP0504 contain empty declared layers; only 46 of 51 English-translated documents also have nonempty transcription | Count actual layer content and document intersections separately from XML markup. |

The seven full-reading reports preserve section summaries, exact disputed unit IDs, credits and provisional examples: [1](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/corpus-reader-1.md), [2](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/corpus-reader-2.md), [3](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/corpus-reader-3.md), [4](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/corpus-reader-4.md), [5](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/corpus-reader-5.md), [6](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/corpus-reader-6.md), [7](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/corpus-reader-7.md). ## Verification and execution

Agent Company mode was chosen because independent grammar, lexical, script, textual and data reviews could run safely in parallel. Seven reviewers were followed by seven corpus readers; at most seven child agents plus the lead ran concurrently. Only the lead edited authoritative knowledge-base files. The expected first review wave was 10–12 minutes (20-minute bound); the full-reading wave was allocated 25 minutes (45-minute bound). First recorded reviewer start was 03:43:05 UTC. Exact per-reader timing and any missing start measurements remain in the reports; no precise token/cost totals are invented.

An independent read-only audit rechecked **5,047 successful manifest entries** against byte lengths and SHA-256. Source originals remain unchanged. The final coverage checks reconcile 4,507 unique IDs without omissions or duplicate assignments. Independent integration QA and Judge evidence are recorded in [the integration review](../sources/reviews/2026-09-24/integration-review.md). These reviews assess this artifact and its evidence, not certify expert Pahlavi proficiency.
