# Documentary readings in Farsi and English

These short examples follow published **transcriptions**. They are source-assisted exercises, not claims of independent decipherment. Farsi translations and grammatical explanations are our study drafts; English renderings follow the cited editors with the stated changes. Source uncertainty remains visible.

## Berk. 25: distinguish giver, recipient and sealer

Source: Asefi 2025, [S23](sources.md#s23), printed p. 9 / PDF 8, lines 6–7 and 9. The first excerpt begins after **kardan abāyēd**, which belongs to the preceding accounting clause.

> may 10 sabōy ō Wahman-Ohrmazd dād

**Farsi:** «[او] ده سبوی شراب به وهمن‌اورمزد داد.»

**English:** “[He] gave Wahman-Ohrmazd ten ewers of wine.”

| Element | Reading in this clause |
|---|---|
| may | wine; می / شراب |
| 10 sabōy | ten ewers; a counted container quantity, without an established litre equivalent here |
| ō Wahman-Ohrmazd | recipient, introduced by ō |
| dād | past form: gave |
| [He] / [او] | supplied grammatical subject; the opening identification is damaged/uncertain |

The later closing formula is:

> čak Dēnabzūd āwišt

**Farsi:** «دین‌افزود سند را مهر کرد.»

**English:** “Dēnabzūd sealed the document.”

Here **čak** is the document being sealed, **Dēnabzūd** is the sealer and **āwišt** is the past verb. The editor uses English “check”; our “document” avoids importing a modern banking sense. The Persian spellings of personal names are editorial renderings of the cited transcription. Do not assign the giver's role to the sealer merely because the sealer is clearly named.

## TB8: an address and greeting

Source: Asefi and Farridnejad 2026, [S25](sources.md#s25), PDF 10–11 / printed 8–9, opening of line 1. The excerpt stops before the month name.

> ō xwadāy Dādfrāy namāz Dādag drōd was

**Farsi:** «به سرور دادفرای(?)، ادای احترام؛ [از] دادگ(?)، درود فراوان.»

**English:** “To lord Dādfrāy(?), reverence; [from] Dādag(?), many greetings.”

The introduction calls the recipient's name likely and the scribe/author's name possible, even though the transcription prints them without question marks. Our translations carry that uncertainty. **[from]** is supplied to express the letter formula; no independent **az** appears in this excerpt.

**xwadāy** addresses a human superior here; “God” would change the addressee. **namāz** expresses respect in this formula; the familiar modern ritual sense is not required. **was** qualifies the greetings. The paper supplies the first six lines of this 24-line letter, not a complete translation of the whole witness.

## Berk. 11: check the quantities independently

Source: [S23](sources.md#s23), PDF 20–21 / printed 21–22. The revised reading describes four donkeys, fifteen days, and daily allotments of two kabīz of barley and two bundles of lucerne **per animal**.

| Check | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Barley | 4 × 15 × 2 kabīz | 120 kabīz |
| Convert using the article's regional ratio | 120 ÷ 10 kabīz per grīw | 12 grīw |
| Lucerne | 4 × 15 × 2 bundles | 120 bundles |

The transcription's **jaw grīw 12** agrees with this calculation. The published English sentence says “twelve kabīz,” while the following commentary explicitly gives twelve grīw. Record this as an edition discrepancy. The calculation checks internal consistency under the author's assumptions; it does not establish a universal grīw-to-kabīz conversion or prove the manuscript reading independently.

**Farsi study summary:** «طبق خوانش پیشنهادی و نسبت واحدها در همین مقاله، جیرهٔ جو برای چهار خر در پانزده روز، در مجموع ۱۲۰ کبیز، برابر با ۱۲ گریو است.»

**English study summary:** “Under the proposed reading and the article's local unit ratio, the barley ration totals 120 kabīz, or 12 grīw.”

## Check understanding

1. Does naming Dēnabzūd establish that he gave the wine? **No.** The quoted closing clause identifies the sealer.
2. Does namāz require “ritual prayer” in TB8? **No.** The letter's address formula supports reverence or respect.
3. Can an edition's English translation override its conflicting numeral or unit without review? **No.** Retain the conflict and inspect the other evidence.
4. Are the two Nyberg PDFs independent support for a disputed reading? **No.** They represent the same manual in different copies.
