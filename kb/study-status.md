## Remaining gaps

1. Complete Skjærvø, MacKenzie and Durkin-Meisterernst references remain unread; Skjærvø retrieval failed again. Most Nyberg entries and the supplied Persian primer's history/reader remain unstudied.
2. Full word annotations, manuscript decipherment, critical-apparatus collation and verified font conversion remain incomplete.
3. Most content in the other collected archives has not been read comprehensively. The data collection itself retains the [recorded access/extraction limits](../data/README.md).
4. Exact-edition conflicts, polarity/numeral differences, cross-unit translations, quoted languages and fragments require resolution. Reading completion is not semantic validation.
5. No expert-scored blind assessment establishes Farsi or English translation accuracy. Already studied examples are not independent test items.

## Assessment

The knowledge base now supports substantially broader source-assisted reading and more careful translation reasoning. It does **not** establish complete language knowledge, unaided manuscript fluency or a reliable production translator. No permanent model training was performed.

Next study should resolve the documented high-impact disagreements using critical editions, deepen dictionary/manual coverage and obtain independent specialist assessment. Preserve original readings and uncertainties; do not manufacture missing translations or promote a plausible analysis to fact.
