# Sources and access status

Initial consultation **20 September 2026**, with a documented review on **24 September 2026**. “Read” below means the stated relevant material, not necessarily an entire work. Published pages and identified editions are the evidence; links and local PDFs provide access. File identity and coverage are recorded in [the source manifest](../sources/manifest.json). The [current reading ledger](../sources/parsig-live-inventory.json) supersedes historical counts in dated notes.

Additional Python tools and associated papers are listed with individual study status in the [resource library](../resources/README.md), [credits](../CREDITS.md) and [bibliography](../resources/references.bib). Those entries do not imply installation or full-paper study.

## Read and used

### S1

Nicholas Sims-Williams, [Ideographic Writing i: Terminology and Conventions](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/ideographic-writing-i-terminology-and-conventions/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 2004. Read for heterograms and transliteration conventions.

### S2

Desmond Durkin-Meisterernst, [Huzwāreš](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/huzwares/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 2004. Read for the writing convention, phonetic complements, and limitations of historical glossary evidence.

### S3

Mauro Maggi and Paola Orsatti, “From Old to New Persian,” in Anousha Sedighi and Pouneh Shabani-Jadidi, eds., *The Oxford Handbook of Persian Linguistics*, 2018. [Publisher-distributed preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780191056413_A35505953/preview-9780191056413_A35505953.pdf). Read printed pp. **18–26**, corresponding to PDF pages **41–49**; inspected phoneme and verb tables visually. This is a 61-page preview, not the complete handbook.

### S4

[Universal Dependencies: Middle Persian](https://universaldependencies.org/pal/index.html). Read the corpus contributors' morphology and syntax documentation. Treat annotation labels as the project's analysis, not automatically as universal grammatical terminology.

### S5

Ali Ashraf Sadeghi and John R. Perry, [Eżāfa](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/ezafa/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 1999. Used for historical linkage; most examples concern New Persian.

### S6

[ASPIRANTUM: Introduction to Middle Persian Grammar and History](https://aspirantum.com/curriculum/middle-persian-pahlavi-syllabus/introduction-to-middle-persian-grammar-and-history). Read the introductory syllabus's pronoun inventory and grammatical overview. Cross-check simplified teaching statements against specialist sources.

### S7

John R. Payne, [Ergative Construction](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/ergative-construction/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 1998. Read for participant roles and alignment.

### S8

Gernot L. Windfuhr, [Cases](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/cases/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 1990. Consulted for the distinction between morphology, syntax, and semantic roles, and the Middle Persian discussion.

### S9

[TITUS: Kārnāmag ī Ardaxšīr ī Pābagān, prologue](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/etcs/iran/miran/mpers/kap/kap001.htm) and [chapter I](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/etcs/iran/miran/mpers/kap/kap002.htm). Data entry D. N. MacKenzie; additions Elio Provasi; TITUS version Jost Gippert. Read the prologue and opening chapter page; analysed I.1, I.15, and I.20. This is edited transcription, not manuscript access.

### S10

[University of Texas: Middle Persian](https://sites.la.utexas.edu/persian_online_resources/history-of-the-language/middle-persian/). Read the writing-system explanation and Bundahišn excerpt. Used the short excerpt for a spelling-to-reading comparison; not as an independent critical edition.

### S11

D. N. MacKenzie, [Frahang ī Pahlawīg](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/frahang-i-pahlawig/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 2000. Read for glossary tradition, selected lexical examples, and problems of attestation.

### S12

Abe Meyers, [Proposal for Encoding Book Pahlavi in the Unicode Standard](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2014/14077-book-pahlavi.pdf), version 1.1, 5 May 2014. Read selected script sections; visually inspected printed p. 11 and fig. 4.2 on p. 38. An encoding proposal, not a statement of current Unicode adoption.

### S13

Carlo Giovanni Cereti, [Middle Persian i: Pahlavi Literature](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/middle-persian/pahlavi-literature/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 2009. Consulted the overview and selected discussions of the Dēnkard and Bundahišn.

### S14

Shaul Shaked, [Gētīg and Mēnōg](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/getig-and-menog/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, 2001. Read for semantic and theological context.

### S15

Darab Dastur Peshotan Sanjana, [Kārnāmag translation](https://www.avesta.org/pahlavi/karname.htm), 1896, hosted by Avesta.org. Compared the opening narrative and the passages used in the worked readings. Historical translation; its orthography, interpretation, and numbering need comparison with the modern edition.

### S16

[FU Berlin: Zoroastrian Middle Persian Corpus and Dictionary](https://www.geschkult.fu-berlin.de/en/e/iranistik/forschung/MPCD/index.html). Read the institutional project description. It describes a manuscript-based annotated corpus and dictionary project. The linked live corpus returned HTTP 403 in this session; no live dictionary queries or corpus extraction were completed.

### S17

Additional specialist context: [Manichaean Script](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/manichean-script/), [Pahlavi Psalter](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/pahlavi-psalter/), [Iranian Languages: Documentation](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/iran-vi2-documentation/), [Early New Persian](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/persian-language-1-early-new-persian/), and [Kār-nāmag](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/karnamag-i-ardasir/), *Encyclopaedia Iranica*. Consulted for the specific distinctions cited in the notes.

### S18

Farhan Farsi, Parnian Fazel, Sepand Haghighi, Sadra Sabouri, Farzaneh Goshtasb, Nadia Hajipour, Ehsaneddin Asgari and Hossein Sameti, [ParsiPy: NLP Toolkit for Historical Persian Texts in Python](https://aclanthology.org/2025.alp-1.17/), ALP 2025, DOI 10.18653/v1/2025.alp-1.17. All 13 [PDF pages](https://aclanthology.org/2025.alp-1.17.pdf) read; selected illustrations checked visually. Bibliographic discrepancy retained: the Anthology landing page gives pp. 137–149, while the retrieved PDF prints pp. 238–250. Use PDF page numbers for our internal locators. This is a toolkit paper, not a completed translation system. The Anthology states CC BY 4.0 for post-2016 materials.

Read the complete letter. Primary author statement hosted by a community site. The database was initially inaccessible; live study is now recorded under S21.

### S20
