Retain these attributions when reusing the notes. Preserve each source's own notice and terms with any redistributed source material. Software licenses do not replace the separate provenance of editions, translations, images or externally hosted datasets.

## Scholarship revisited on 24 September 2026

The new grammar checks credit **Thomas Jügel's 2015 English Summary**, Maggi and Orsatti's historical grammar discussion, Nyberg's selected grammar/glossary pages, and the UD/MPCD contributors. The writing and vocabulary reviews also credit **Nicholas Sims-Williams, Desmond Durkin-Meisterernst, Shaul Shaked and Philippe Gignoux**. Exact source sections and reading limits are recorded in [the source register](kb/sources.md) and the review reports under `sources/reviews/2026-09-24/`.

The new short-text readings preserve Parsig's edition and translator credits, including **Jamasp-Asana, Farzaneh Goshtasb, Nadia Hajipour and Raham Asha**. The connected Kārnāmag study compares **MacKenzie's TITUS transcription, with Provasi/Gippert electronic contributions**, against **Sanjana's 1896 English**. The 34-page Xusrō/page edition credits **Raham Asha** and retains its copyright notice. Our new Farsi/English renderings and analyses are explicitly provisional; the source scholars are not credited with or held responsible for those new renderings.
