## Frozen scientific choice

Five fixed epochs, microbatch4, accumulated groups of16 parents, final13-parent group per epoch retained: 11,185 exposures, 700 updates. Forty-two warmup updates followed by linear decay. Seed3407 and fixed reproducible per-epoch permutations. Target-token CE pooled over all unmasked tokens in the entire accumulated update; no label smoothing, reweighting, packing or truncation. Preserve checkpoint dropout. Final update700 is the sole primary checkpoint, no DEV-driven stop/selection.
