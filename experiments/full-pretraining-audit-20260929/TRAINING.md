## Finding TRAIN-001 — P2: completed training is misreported after evaluation failure

`cloud_pilot/hf_mixed.py:127–157` initializes `training_schedule_completed=False` and changes it to true only after `mixed_body` returns. That body runs training, reloads the model, and completes DEV evaluation before returning. A reload/evaluation failure after all 96 training updates therefore exports `training_schedule_completed=False`, even when the final adapter and inner training receipt are complete. This is a phase-accounting and recovery defect; it does not establish any defect in the completed candidate's optimization or explain its translation regression.

**Reproduction:** `training_check.py` calls the actual `execute_mixed` function with inert setup, a synthetic completed 96-step/1,536-slot inner training receipt, and an injected subsequent evaluation exception. The original exception propagates. This fixture demonstrates the wrapper's phase-conflation; it does not pretend its synthetic receipt alone qualifies an adapter.

**Minimum guarded repair, owned by root:** extract a completed-training validator, shared by `completed_mixed` and failure finalization. Require the closed training receipt's completed status, exactly 96 steps/1,536 slots, fixed settings, fresh optimizer, admitted and changed step-20 canary, and verified parent order. Compare all ordered IDs with `pilot.selected_ids_in_order` from the checksum-verified copied data manifest; bind outer runner/data/script identities to the frozen settings. Require nonempty config and safetensors entries, exact adapter inventory, and each file's recorded checksum. Set only `training_schedule_completed=True` after that validator passes. Keep overall mixed status incomplete and evaluation false, and preserve the original error. Missing or invalid evidence must leave training unconfirmed. Do not infer completion from existence of a file or a boolean alone.

The new validator must be included in `hf_mixed.prepare`'s `inspect.getsource` serialization tuple (`hf_mixed.py:212–214`), before every serialized caller.
