## Interpretation and limits

The new a-dart lookup is actual definition evidence; the exact source-to-Persian span alignments are author proposals that still require independent review. In509000008 the eight target bracket characters and ear-address explanation remain intact; in513000002 the opening/closing ellipses remain intact. A local noun can be reviewed within a fragment, but no missing material or complete-clause reliability follows. If that scope is not independently accepted, omit it rather than filling the slot automatically;151014029 is only an explicitly identified simpler backup, not an admitted replacement.

The pain-free/bare-noun comparison is the clearest local contrast to the old afflicted predicate. The taking examples demonstrate that the lexical family need not occur only after ma; the noun example also prevents treating every appar substring as the same command. These are candidate construction controls, not matched experimental pairs: works, lengths, genres, participants and qualifications differ. Selecting a training mixture requires a separate exposure-controlled design and admission decision. No held-out reference, model output, paid computation, network, credential, source-code edit or new corpus acquisition was used.
