Likelihood: per-parent mismatch penalty = NLL(mismatched) − NLL(correct), in nats per supervised token. Report every pair, sign counts, equal-parent mean/median/range and by-work summaries. Content-only summaries exclude exactly the declared initial language tag and final EOS; keep full training-token summaries primary. These are diagnostics, never translation-merit scores or inferred accuracy. Unpaired sources are not expert-certified semantic negatives; their lengths and vocabulary differ. Gold prefixes supply strong information. No significance or causal mechanism claim.

Generation: two fresh blind provisional reviewers use the unchanged semantic rubric and inherited source/reference qualifications; report20-case TRAIN coverage/failures and local scoped findings separately. Reliable familiar translation alongside poor held-out DEV would prioritize coverage/representation/transfer. Poor generation with weak source separation would reopen conditioning/learnability. Mixed evidence preserves uncertainty and does not automatically admit another run.

Prediction before the new outputs: partial familiar lexical recovery with persistent compositional errors. Existing evidence includes known TRAIN words translated incorrectly, unseen compounds/transcription variants in work-held-out DEV, and fluent semantic drift in uncapped outputs. Source counts are descriptive, not philological normalization rules. Ordinary extra epochs, beam search, blanket normalization and another model are not selected by this evidence alone.
