## Assessment of the new external critique

The user-supplied report is retained verbatim in [external-review.txt](external-review.txt). Its stated inspection limits matter: it did not inspect every underlying artifact. Its broad warning against an uncontrolled train-and-retest loop is useful; its causal diagnosis is too strong.

| Claim or recommendation | Assessment after checking actual records |
| --- | --- |
| A universal 20-fold gradient bug invalidates all training | Unsupported. `contextual_train.py` deliberately uses equal-example loss, as declared before the experiment. With accumulation 16, each example's mean loss has coefficient 1/16; a token in an answer of length N has coefficient 1/(16N). Five-token versus 100-token answers therefore differ 20-fold per token, not per example. This is not a measured gradient-norm ratio. The native-loss CPU probe matched equal-example loss, while NLLB uses a separate pooled-token loss. Weighting is a legitimate experimental choice to revisit, not an established implementation error. [Declared census](../contextual-supervision-20260927/token-census/REPORT.md), [native probe](../full-pretraining-audit-20260929/training-audit.json). |

## Why a much larger resource has not yielded a much better translator

The corrected resource contains **9,973 unique prompts**: 2,232 historical Persian translations; 7,438 lexical inventories (2,606 Persian, 3,412 MacKenzie English and 1,420 Manichaean English); 240 conditioned teaching units; 53 documentary English spans; six Persian inscription occurrences; and four English edition spans. Counts and bounded qualification are in the [corrected release](../training-ready-v2-20260929/README.md). They are not 9,973 independently validated Persian sentence translations.

The latest run used 1,152 historical, 192 lexical and 192 other examples per cycle. Thus it cannot estimate the full pool's benefit. Conversely, simply presenting every pool row once under equal-example weighting assigns about 74.6% of example terms to lexical tasks, versus the pilot's 12.5%. That is a large objective change. Keeping two lexical examples in every batch of 16 while covering all 7,438 lexical prompts requires at least 3,719 batches and 59,504 exposures, including 44,628 historical slots. This arithmetic assumes that exact fixed batch mix; it is not a required training schedule or a time estimate.

**All reliable information should be available, but it need not all be memorized with identical weight.** Preserve the complete structured archive and expose qualified dictionary evidence through a local lookup resource. For learning, derive concise task-specific views, keep every supported sense/qualifier and train on independently justified contextual examples. English glosses remain English evidence; automatically translating them does not create published Persian gold. Historical passages are not automatically suitable word alignments. Latin scholarly reading, native-sign transcription and uncertain decipherment remain separate tasks.

JSON overhead is a valid efficiency and task-design concern. However, five of six taught structured inventories were learned, so JSON cannot be declared the demonstrated root cause. A concise projection must remain reversible to its source inventory; typography, apparatus, homographs, compound meanings and uncertainty must not be flattened away.

## What related research changes

These studies motivate hypotheses, not Pahlavi performance predictions. The prior research inventory is preserved in [linguistic structure](../../output/research-next-step-20260928/LINGUISTIC-STRUCTURE.md), [learning objectives](../../output/research-next-step-20260928/LEARNING-OBJECTIVES.md), [learning regimes](../dev-assisted-qualified-20260927/RESEARCH-REGIMES-UPDATE.md) and [institutional workflows](../dev-assisted-qualified-20260927/RESEARCH-INSTITUTIONS-METHODS.md).

- **Source-aligned linguistic evidence is a credible direction.** LingoLLM combines dictionary entries and morphological analysis for low-resource translation; some ablations depend on oracle analyses. This supports testing correct supplied analysis before investing in an analyzer, not assuming automatic Pahlavi analysis works. [LingoLLM, ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.925.pdf), especially section 5.
- **Representation matters.** Ancient-Greek interlinear research reports benefits from some morphology encodings, while directly inserting tags performs worse than baseline on average. Therefore neither a generic grammar prompt nor extra JSON tags are validated solutions. Its aligned New Testament task differs from cross-work Persian translation. [Greek study, 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.loreslm-1.11.pdf), Table 6.
- **Use existing linguistic tools as candidates.** ParsiPy provides analysis of transcribed Middle Persian, including lemma/POS and conversion functions. Its output requires compatibility and accuracy checks before becoming labels. PahGen goes from English to Pahlavi with grammar and dictionary support; its mostly constructed short-sentence evaluation is not historical Pahlavi-to-Persian gold. [ParsiPy](https://aclanthology.org/2025.alp-1.17/), [PahGen](https://aclanthology.org/2025.loresmt-1.16.pdf).
- **Evidence access can help scholars without proving autonomous translation.** Aeneas supplies inscription parallels and predictions for historical tasks. Our earlier nearest-example assistance increased serious errors, so the next test needs occurrence-specific support for the decisive relation, not another bag of related passages. [Aeneas, Nature 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09292-5.pdf).
- **Do not infer the next learning regime from symptoms alone.** Minimum-risk training has reduced some translation hallucinations in larger modern-language studies; other work finds limited incremental exposure-bias effects in language generation. Neither diagnoses this project. Scheduled sampling also has a published inconsistency critique. [MT study](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.326/), [self-recovery study](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.415/), [scheduled-sampling analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05101).
- **Synthetic diversity is not automatically useful.** Controlled low-resource augmentation research finds that some linguistically valid changes worsen performance. Prefer additional authentic contextual examples; do not pad the corpus with low-confidence labels. [ACL 2025 augmentation study](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1307.pdf).

CPT, backtranslation, DPO/CPO, alternative adapters and model changes remain conditional possibilities. None currently has the independently qualified corpus, reverse translator, reward or local controlled result needed to outrank the narrower next question. A new grammar or dictionary download is not automatically new independent sentence supervision.

**First proposed paid experiment, after those prerequisites:** one existing-checkpoint inference pilot asking, *Can correct supplied lexical and grammatical evidence enable faithful composition?* Use retained Gemma step280, one pinned numerical path and tokenizer, one decoding policy, equal output limits and one first attempt per condition:

1. Source alone.
2. Source plus complete qualified dictionary senses.
3. The same source and dictionary evidence plus independently checked occurrence-specific grammatical relations and scope.

A planning cap is 24 short passages across at least four work families, giving at most 72 new outputs in one model load. This is a feasible-size target for qualification, not an already assembled set, a power calculation or a declaration of 24 independent observations. Select by predeclared source/structure strata, not known model success. Use material separated from adaptation and previous evaluation where provenance supports that claim; if eligibility cannot be established, label the pilot as familiar development diagnosis and do not advertise unseen transfer. Do not inspect reserve answers to choose easy items.

The 72-output cap covers only this new three-condition pilot. No legacy DEV/PAL rerun is included or required to re-establish unchanged step280 performance. Existing legacy outputs remain historical evidence; any later new candidate must face the unchanged regression gates under its own explicit run budget. Do not silently add generations to this pilot.

Admission also requires an explicit information-difference audit against S04 lexical hints, S07 grammar notes and the 58-attachment assistance experiment. Each new relation must link an actual source span to independently supported participants, scope or morphology. If the packet is only those earlier generic aids reformatted, cancel this proposed pilot rather than paying to repeat it.

The third condition is an **assisted upper-bound test**, not autonomous translation. Its added information is deliberate, so length and information content are not controlled separately; do not attribute any improvement uniquely to prompt formatting. Annotation must come from published source analysis or a qualified independent annotator, without reading candidate outputs or deriving relations from the target translation. Where only the published translation supports an analysis, hold that item from this experiment. A blind assessor receives the output and qualified reference evidence, not the condition identity. Generic self-generated reasoning is not a substitute for a verified analysis.

Before generation, freeze exact case IDs, uncertainty rules, component denominators, the paired acceptance decision, critical-error constraints and cross-work requirement. Retain the old fixed merit as a separate regression measure; do not pool it with the new pilot. The old two-net-gain/cross-work screen remains an operational legacy screen, not a significance test. The supplementary pilot's decision rule and any precision calculation must be finalized before seeing its outputs. If qualification fails, stop before paying; seek missing annotation or better sources rather than filling slots with guesses.

This pilot is selected for its information value and absence of new optimization, not because all alternatives are disproved. The strongest alternative for a **causal training** question is already documented: matched auxiliary-on versus auxiliary-off continuation with identical historical rows/order, initialization, schedule and historical loss coefficient. For the old 12+4 batch, use `[sum(history losses) + w*sum(auxiliary losses)]/16`, w=0 versus 1; renormalizing the control over 12 changes the historical dose. That experiment distinguishes the selected auxiliary contribution from more continuation, but does not establish the value of a new representation or full corpus. Do not run both branches automatically. [Existing design](../learning-diagnosis-20260929/REPORT.md).

Any later training proposal must encompass the full qualified resource inventory before selecting its objective and exact exposure schedule. It must state what is available through lookup, what is actually learned, what stays excluded, and why. If new concise formatting, contextual data and sampling are changed together, label the experiment as a combined recipe comparison; do not claim one factor caused the outcome. A lower learning rate or token-normalized loss is an untested intervention, not an automatic repair. Independent Astra review must cover the actual data/configuration and prediction before launch.
