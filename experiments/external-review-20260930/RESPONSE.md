## What we have been doing wrong

1. **We allowed another training run before answering the missing learning question.** The 28-prompt acquisition diagnostic existed but had not run when the corrected-data pilot was launched. Repairing contradictory labels was justified; paying to compare repaired training before checking auxiliary acquisition left the same central ambiguity. Did the model fail to learn the selected tasks, fail to apply them in context, or respond badly to the interface? Repeated data audits and passage scores cannot answer that. Recording a caveat is not completing the measurement.
2. **Dataset size was too prominent compared with actual exposure and relevance.** The corrected pool has 9,973 unique inputs, but the pilot consumed only 1,536 examples, including 192 lexical tasks. The correct dictionary senses of `zahag` exist in the qualified pool but their entries were not selected in either recent run; no bounded `zahag`/`zahāg` form match was found in either selected source stream. Its persistent DEV error therefore cannot demonstrate that these continuations learned and ignored that entry. This does not license adding a DEV-targeted fix to TRAIN. Future coverage must be measured over an independently chosen training-side frame.
3. **Evaluation has not yet reached the requested scientific standard.** Fifteen whole passages from four works, repeatedly inspected, with provisional AI judgments and no independent specialist adjudication of this benchmark are useful regression evidence. They are insufficient for a reliable population-quality claim or for ranking small improvements. The corrected candidate's one extra acceptance does not establish general improvement. Two same-family reviewers add procedural checking, not independent philological validation.
4. **Some experimental explanations have outrun their controls.** Both mixed runs restart an optimizer/schedule from step280. Comparing either with step280 confounds extra optimization with added tasks. Comparing the two sibling mixed runs is more tightly matched, but still changes several corrected inputs and some selected records together. English task mix, terminator weighting, editorial style and missing context remain competing hypotheses, not established causes.

The appropriate correction is a binding measurement order, not another broad data-cleaning loop or automatic model switch.

## Research used to check the recommendations

- [Hugging Face chat templates](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/chat_templating): message roles become model tokens and training/generation formatting matters. This supports checking exact prefixes; it does not show the project's wrapper is the dominant cause.
- [Dettmers et al., QLoRA, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314): adapters are fitted through a frozen quantized backbone. This motivates a numerical sensitivity check, not a claim that BF16 evaluation is inherently invalid.
- [Vamvas and Sennrich, 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.blackboxnlp-1.5/): minimal-pair evaluation can give false positives and differ from deployment behavior. This is why contrastive likelihood remains supplementary to actual translation.
- [Freitag et al., 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.87/): professional, context-aware error assessment changed MT rankings relative to crowd evaluation. This motivates specialist calibration; its modern-language results do not establish a Pahlavi-specific sample size or expected improvement.

All four primary pages were checked on 30 September 2026. No claim of new Pahlavi model performance is taken from them.
