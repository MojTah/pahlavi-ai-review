## Main finding

The review identifies a real measurement gap: the latest added supervision was evaluated on complete translations without first measuring acquisition of its own auxiliary tasks. It overstates that gap if it says the test cannot show that training helps, treats 100% binary acceptance agreement as proof of nonindependence, or calls 1/15 versus 2/15 equivalent. The existing reports already disclose repeated DEV exposure, few works, provisional references, same-family AI raters, and lack of statistical or generalization claims. The governance issue is converting those acknowledged limits into a binding next measurement decision, rather than paying for another recipe with the same unresolved intermediate question.

## Specific appraisal of the150–300 likelihood-foil proposal

This is a reasonable **prospective source-conditioned discrimination diagnostic**, not an already powerful experiment or a measure that directly separates knowing from generating. Its reference-versus-foil fraction should remain supplementary. The old28-prompt packet has low generalization scope by design; that does not make it unable to answer whether the exact exposed auxiliary task can be reproduced. They answer different questions.

Before opening or authoring candidate answers, freeze:

1. **Unit and target.** What semantic distinction, register and source population are covered? One independent attestation/passage family is the unit; variants and several foils from that passage are linked repeated measures. The150–300 pair count alone establishes neither power nor precision. Count independent passages, works, entry families and construction families; select a feasible pilot before promising a final sample size.
2. **Qualification.** A suitably knowledgeable annotator must establish that the reference is supported, the foil is specifically wrong, both are otherwise grammatical/plausible, and no alternate reading makes the foil valid. Human checking without relevant language expertise is not sufficient for every Pahlavi role/sense distinction. Both reference and foil need validation, not merely the edit operation. Preserve unresolved cases under a frozen rule rather than remove them after seeing model scores.
3. **Exact likelihood score.** Freeze prompt, precision, tokenizer, start/end handling, whether the full target or defined contrast span is scored, and length/token normalization. Reference and foil lengths/tokenizations can differ, so raw and per-token likelihood can produce different rankings. Do not select the winning scoring formula after results. Define ties, caps and missing scores. Report the continuous reference-minus-foil margin and per-checkpoint paired changes as well as the binary fraction; identify the primary contrast and handle multiple categories prospectively.
4. **Source-sensitivity control.** A reference can outrank a malformed or improbable Persian foil without any Pahlavi understanding. Include a prespecified no-source or matched mismatched-source scoring control, and examine the change in the gold-versus-foil margin. Balance plausible foil types where possible. A50% binary preference is not automatically a calibrated chance null for a deterministic language model with unequal candidate naturalness. Correct-source preference is evidence of discrimination on those targets, not a proof of semantic understanding.
5. **Exposure separation.** Teacher-forced scoring necessarily presents reference/foil prefixes to the model computation; log that evaluation exposure, isolate cases/checkpoints and never use it for fitting or tuning. Do not describe such scoring as source-only free generation. Keep systematic-error DEV probes in a separately labelled exposed diagnostic stratum; never mix them into a claimed untouched holdout estimate.
6. **Interpretation and feasibility.** High discrimination plus poor generation leaves target-prefix assistance, language priors, scoring design, memorization, source grounding, prompt and decoding as competing explanations. Chance-level preference plus a lexical inventory match does not establish non-transfer: actual selected exposure, qualified sense/context applicability and acquisition have to be verified. The report's under-one-GPU-hour forecast is unverified; target lengths, number of checkpoints/controls and loading determine cost. Annotation may dominate. Refresh actual resource admission before execution.

If all foils cannot be validated, shrinking to known systematic errors is a smaller exploratory study, not an exemption from meaning/foil validation and not preservation of the original power claim. A useful first step is specialist review of the already exposed case009 and the alleged systematic-error meanings; this does not consume previously protected answers. The *zahag* examples themselves are not newly adjudicated by this audit.

## Latent design gaps to keep visible

- **Acquisition versus transfer versus intervention causality are three questions.** The28-record packet answers the first and familiar retention. A held-out probe addresses transfer. Neither by itself identifies whether auxiliary gradients, extra updates, target repairs, English/Persian task mix or sampling caused a change.
- **Binary floor does not justify loosening safety.** All-or-nothing acceptance has little resolution near zero; existing category flags and constrained spans already preserve some partial information. Prospective supported-proposition/role/sense measures can improve diagnostic resolution, but a post-hoc composite or selecting only improved features would create a new moving target.
- **Rater consistency is not validation.** Same-family errors may be shared; new model families may add diversity without providing expertise. Shuffled model identities do not eliminate recognition of repeated source passages or comparison/context effects within one72-record review. Preserve rater disagreement and use expert calibration rather than treating agreement as ground truth.
- **Single seed and one candidate instance limit recipe claims.** Deterministic inference and a shared seed improve matching but do not measure across-seed training variability. Do not pay for replication until the simpler acquisition diagnosis justifies it; still disclose the boundary.
- **Four works are not four representative random draws.** Current work weighting, source lengths and15/9 qualification selection define this convenience diagnostic. Work-count thresholds are sensible safeguards but not inferential replication guarantees. Longer passages offer more opportunities for whole-answer failure; compare paired same passages and report length/complexity strata prospectively for new measurement.
- **Strict contrastive tasks can test recognition instead of generation.** Forced-choice or supplying all plausible senses may make evidence available that a translator would need to infer. Report that condition separately and retain free-generation/source-only translation as its own target.
- **Incomplete acquisition inference can be informative but biased.** Existing launcher readiness explicitly says90 seconds×56 attempts can exceed the proposed computation window. Balance checkpoint/module order and preserve full scheduled denominators; do not interpret an easy completed prefix as complete mastery.
