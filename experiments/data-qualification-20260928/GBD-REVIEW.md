## Rejected false alarms and preserved polysemy

- **GBD90:** unmacronized `azādān` with نازادگان must not be automatically “corrected” into noble/free `āzādān`. Nyberg separately records `a-zād` “unborn” and `āzād` noble/free. No contradiction is established by this comparison; retain the Bahar record.
- **GBD643 versus 625/644:** MacKenzie's German `mēzīdan` entry gives `saugen` (suck), supporting the مکیدن inventory. Do not delete it merely because another source inventory gives a urinary meaning.
- **GBD6:** شاید can be a legitimate modal gloss. No blanket uncertainty rule based on that word is justified, and unfamiliarity with `āxtan` alone is not evidence of an error. An initial tentative hold was withdrawn before freezing this review; the original entry remains untouched.
- Preserve `kištan` cultivation (489) versus `kuštan` killing (589/699), including vowel distinctions. Search-time approximate matches never define admission equivalence.
- Preserve separately published readings for hamār, ēvāz, pēšag, škuftan, vaxš and other homographs. A dictionary listing only one reading does not disprove Bahar's other contextual sense. Voice, grammatical role, deity/epithet use, synonyms and register restrictions are not collapsed into a winning translation.
- Archaic Persian forms, ordinary orthographic conventions and small diacritic differences alone are not sufficient error evidence. The hold map states a concrete concern for each affected scope.
