## Source fidelity and ambiguity

Quoted gloss contents are retained; Persian spacing/ZWNJ and punctuation glyphs are normalized without lexical modernization. Thus the printed دانیانه forms remain as printed. Optional letters, morphological hyphens, literal dots and source variants survive. Where the page joins form alternatives with Persian و or table braces/line breaks, the normalized bundle delimiter is explicitly disclosed in the record. j with caron is encoded as ǰ. The ā in ānō(h/y) was checked explicitly.

For polyfunctional items such as kē, čē, kū, bē, pas, ham and nē, grammatical context determines the record or its attached additional context. This is not a search for one winning translation. Exact same source/target repetitions with additional grammatical uses are consolidated, while different explicit sense inventories remain separate contextual units with shared lexical lineage. All variants and meanings in a printed bundle remain together, without Cartesian expansion.
