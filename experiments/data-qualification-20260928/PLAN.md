## Release question and acceptance meaning

The descriptive question is how much additional source-supported supervision can be qualified, at each evidence type, from the available material. Count complete lexical sense inventories, morphology contexts, pedagogical clauses and authentic aligned passages separately. Rows, spelling variants and alternate editions are not independent attestations. No predicted quality percentage or causal training claim is made here.

Qualified means traceable published source, correct extraction and source/target scope, explicit language/edition/context, preserved uncertainty and alternatives, resolved known defects, and satisfied relevant split/duplication checks. It does not mean error-free or specialist-certified. A source's authority is evidence, not a guarantee. Reviewer agreement cannot override contradictory source evidence.

Generic dictionary definitions may be qualified as lexical sense inventories without pretending they are sentence translations or identifying one context-free winning sense. Keep complete form bundles and grammatical roles; no Cartesian variant/sense expansion. Contextual quotations and attested examples require work/witness clearance separately. Never learn protected-work passages or test-answer corrections, even through alternate editions. A generic vocabulary resource is not evidence of unseen-vocabulary generalization; lexical benchmark applicability must remain explicit before later evaluation.

A target in English/German/French remains in that language. No automatic translation into Persian gold. Apparent polysemy, homography, related glosses, uncertainty, inflection and compound scope have separate fields/decisions. A damaged glyph, missing primary gloss, unresolved form-to-sense relationship, reconstruction presented as certain or unresolved language identity keeps the affected record held. Preserve all raw evidence and the reason for every hold.
