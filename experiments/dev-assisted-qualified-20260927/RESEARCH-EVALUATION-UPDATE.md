## PahGen is useful method evidence, not a replacement checkpoint

The [2025 PahGen paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.loresmt-1.16.pdf), sections3–8, studies **English into Pahlavi** using lexical/morphological processing plus GPT-4o. Its200 test items include167 constructed sentences and33 historical examples, filtered for available vocabulary. Human assessment covers20 items divided between two experts, then negotiated. The paper reports poor direct generation even with vocabulary prompting, and much stronger results from its fuller pipeline. It also acknowledges restricted vocabulary and uncertain generalization beyond handcrafted examples. The360-pair dataset claim is separate from the200-item evaluation; the earlier release-count discrepancy has not been resolved here.

This was already in project research; the fresh check changes its role in the decision, not its novelty. My inference is to test accessible attested evidence now and consider verified lexical/morphological structure if plain examples fail. It does not establish reverse-direction Persian quality, reliable unknown-word discovery or a ready Pahlavi model to swap in. Its character-overlap scores are not comparable to PAL-REF acceptance fractions.

## Two AI reviewers cannot certify the training corpus or model

[Fu and Liu, EMNLP2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.587.pdf), sections3–6 and Table1, study judge consistency across languages/tasks. They find important language-dependent inconsistency and no simple guarantee from model scale or multilingual tuning. This is not an experiment on our specific judge, Pahlavi or current reference-assisted rubric; do not import its agreement numbers as our error rate.

A [2026 survey of multilingual/low-resource LLM judging](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02235v1), sections4–5, emphasizes target-language validation and human checks. Its concern directly applies to interpreting our evidence: reference availability and two same-family agents reduce some procedural weaknesses but do not prove philological competence. Every training row was screened, yet the qualification remains AI-assisted. The modest paired gain may contain rater uncertainty; it must not be marketed as exact model accuracy or proof that all excluded translations were wrong.

Keep all published references, uncertainty flags, frozen reviews and reviewer disagreements. Before research-grade delivery or claiming newly decoded meanings, obtain a documented Pahlavi-specialist check of representative judgments and difficult readings; do not replace that with more same-family votes. No specialist contact, payment or new review occurred in this task. This missing validation need not block a clearly provisional development experiment, but it limits what its result can justify.

## How to avoid remaining in one approach

The present result demonstrates successful training execution, not a proven optimization plateau or global optimum. Cleaning changed row count, token exposure and training schedule together; small gains plus per-work regressions do not identify the best backbone, recipe or language representation.

The next comparison should cross two dimensions: **current adapted model versus one credible different-family unadapted challenger**, and **source alone versus the same qualified evidence package**. Keep the24 existing DEV cases and15whole/nineconstrained assessment; do not pick an easier subset or select individual PAL-REF failures. This is96 first-attempt outputs, without retraining either model. It compares usable conditions, not architecture causally, because adaptation histories differ. A weaker unadapted challenger does not prove it could never improve after adaptation.

Use each model's native tokenizer/template and freeze its execution policy before outputs. Matching message content does not mean matching token IDs across model families. Explicitly disclose thinking/sampling differences if native recommendations require them, and keep each model's policy identical between plain and assisted arms. Do not silently cripple or optimize one side, search prompts after seeing results, or rerun selectively. The final choice of challenger and numerical runtime settings still needs the separate model review/readiness check.

A clear challenger win opens a model switch; a clear assistance gain opens an evidence-supported method; broadly weak results redirect work toward trusted word senses/constructions and reference validation. Mixed results remain tradeoffs. No single small pilot guarantees the global best solution, and no branch automatically authorizes a sweep of models or repeated training. The unchanged PAL-REF set remains a later regression check after a development choice is fixed.
