# One model-family challenge before further training

Research date: 2026-09-27. Primary-source document review only. No weights, packages, model calls, credentials, or cloud resources were used. This is a proposed diagnostic, not a replacement decision or an expert assessment of Pahlavi.

**Recommendation: test `Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B`, unchanged, on the same 24 genuine DEV items under plain and qualified-evidence conditions.** It is a verified current dense 27B model, rather than an assumed Qwen3.5 name or a small-to-large ladder. Selection is provisional: the useful experiment asks whether a different general model can interpret unfamiliar linguistic evidence better. Published sources do not establish that Qwen is better at Pahlavi than Gemma or Hy-MT2.

## Evidence and artifacts

The [official Qwen3.6-27B card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B) identifies Apache-2.0 licensing, a dense 27B language model with vision encoder, native 262,144 context, and text-only serving with vLLM `--language-model-only`. It recommends vLLM >=0.19.0, SGLang >=0.5.10, or current Transformers. These are documentation claims; the project's pinned runtime was not tested for compatibility.

The [official repository files](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B/tree/main) total 55.6 GB: fifteen `model-00001-of-00015.safetensors` through `model-00015-of-00015.safetensors` shards plus `model.safetensors.index.json`. The first shard is 3.97 GB and last 509 MB. Tokenizer assets are `tokenizer.json` (12.8 MB), `tokenizer_config.json` (16.7 kB), `vocab.json` (6.72 MB), `merges.txt` (3.35 MB), and `chat_template.jinja` (7.76 kB). Sizes are repository display values, not local verified bytes. Live main displayed short revision `6a9e13b`; a full immutable revision and checksums still need freezing before execution.

Its [actual config](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B/blob/main/config.json) declares `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration`, model type `qwen3_5`, BF16 text weights, 64 language layers, 248,320 vocabulary slots and alternating linear/full attention. [Transformers' own architecture documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/main/en/model_doc/qwen3_5) explicitly provides `Qwen3_5ForCausalLM` with `Qwen3_5TextConfig` for text-only generation. Loading the complete multimodal checkpoint through that text-only class and checking weight mappings remains an implementation smoke test; do not simply reuse Gemma's loader or tokenizer.

The language evidence is **family coverage, not Pahlavi competence**. Qwen's [3.5 announcement](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.5) explicitly lists Persian, Dari and Tajik; its [3.5 model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-27B) states 201 languages/dialects. Unsloth's [3.6 implementation guide](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.6) also states 201-language coverage. I found no Pahlavi training disclosure, direct Pahlavi benchmark, or Persian-specific quality comparison for this exact 3.6 checkpoint in the inspected sources. Modern Persian coverage cannot establish Middle Persian understanding.

## Comparison with the incumbent and specialist

The [Gemma4-31B official card](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-31B-it) describes 30.7B parameters, 256K context, over 140 languages and configurable thinking. Qwen is a comparable-scale family challenge; it does not obviously dominate Gemma from these specifications. The parent's approximately 40–42.5% provisional PALREF result for qualified Gemma LoRA280 is project context, not independently rechecked here. General reasoning/coding leaderboard results cannot predict Pahlavi meaning accuracy; different inference modes further limit such comparisons.

[Hy-MT2-30B-A3B](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-MT2-30B-A3B) is a serious alternative, not an obsolete 7B strawman: Tencent explicitly lists Persian, translation specialization, context and terminology instructions, Apache-2.0, and full/LoRA tuning. Its recommended 30B inference settings are temperature 0.7, top-p 1.0, top-k -1, repetition penalty 1.0 and 4,096 output tokens. Its documented Transformers path needs >=5.6.0 and `trust_remote_code=True`.

The [Tencent training guide](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-MT2/tree/main/train) specifically names `hy_v3_lora_sft.yaml`, a `hy_v3` template, rank64/alpha128 attention-projection LoRA defaults, and 8,192-token suggested LoRA cutoff. This is stronger specialist adaptation evidence than a generic assertion that any model accepts LoRA. The [official model table](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-MT2) lists GGUF artifacts for 1.8B and 7B, but lists 30B-A3B only in full and FP8 forms; I did not establish an official 30B GGUF/offload path. This is an unverified deployment detail, not proof of incompatibility or inferior quality.

Why choose Qwen first: the immediate unknown is transfer from explanatory evidence into an unsupported ancient language. A broad general model with explicit reasoning/direct-answer modes and documented dense text adaptation makes a useful independent challenge to Gemma. That is an experiment-design hypothesis, not evidence that dense models inherently learn languages better than MoE or that specialists cannot use examples. Hy-MT2's context interface makes it plausible too. If Qwen fails, it would not settle Hy-MT2 or all other families; avoid treating one negative result as evidence that only the data matters.

## Runtime and later adaptation feasibility

Unsloth's [3.6 guide](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.6) supplies llama.cpp usage and explicitly routes training to its 3.5 guide. It lists about 30 GB combined memory for 8-bit and 55 GB for BF16. Its published [Qwen3.6-27B-Q8_0.gguf](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-GGUF/blob/main/Qwen3.6-27B-Q8_0.gguf) is 28.6 GB with displayed SHA256 `f93f517f38e696d35a1a7df2c0e3155a64f4c4dcd662107a146ae263f7fb14ce`. This is a quantizer-published conversion, not Qwen's original safetensors; local bytes were not downloaded or checked.

Inference: Q8 is a plausible fit for approximately 64 GB RAM with partial 8 GB GPU offload, leaving room for a bounded context and runtime buffers. It cannot reside completely in 8 GB VRAM. BF16 inference on one A10080 is plausible at batch1 with bounded context. Neither memory fit nor latency was measured, and advertised maximum context is not a promise of fitting it on this hardware. Preserve a BF16 reference for the capability test if root's budget plan allows; evaluate eventual Q8 quality separately.

The [Unsloth fine-tuning guide](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.5/fine-tune) explicitly supports dense27B text LoRA/QLoRA, publishes an A10080 notebook, and reports about56 GB for BF16 LoRA. Its text example targets q/k/v/o and gate/up/down projections with checkpointing. Together with the explicit3.6 cross-reference, this supports a credible later tuning route, not a reproduced project recipe or a measured3.6 memory guarantee. Local8GB training is not established. Gemma adapters cannot be reused on Qwen; tokenization, labels, layer targeting and export need independent checks. Do not train before the inference diagnostic.

## Proposed diagnostic and interpretation

Use Qwen's official nonthinking instruction mode as the prespecified primary operational condition: `enable_thinking=false` in the chat template, temperature0.7, top-p0.8, top-k20, min-p0, presence penalty1.5, repetition penalty1.0. Qwen defaults to thinking and does not support the old `/nothink` soft switch. Its general thinking recipe instead uses temperature1.0, top-p0.95, top-k20 and presence penalty0. The card recommends32,768 output tokens generally. [Official mode documentation](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B).

For completeness, [Qwen3.5's official card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-27B) supports the same general nonthinking settings and template switch. Its general thinking presence penalty is1.5, unlike3.6's0. Its nonthinking-reasoning recommendations differ between two card sections; that inconsistency does not affect the general nonthinking recipe proposed here. Do not transplant settings between versions silently.

1. Freeze the exact24 DEV IDs before viewing challenger answers. Keep DEV/PALREF/TEST reference answers out of prompts and retrieval. Evidence comes only from the frozen qualified TRAIN material and already permitted source documentation; identical-source/near-parallel leakage exclusions remain necessary.
2. Generate24 plain and24 evidence-assisted Qwen outputs. Reuse the incumbent's24+24 results only if inputs/evidence were frozen identically; otherwise the full comparison is96 outputs. Use the same evidence text across families, with model-specific token counts and chat templates recorded.
3. Freeze sampling seed(s), evidence ordering, effective parameters, output cap and stopping rules before scoring. A4096 output cap can be a declared translation-specific operational choice, but inspect finish reasons and never score truncation as a capability failure without reporting it. It differs from the vendor's general32K recommendation. Thinking would be a separate, explicitly budgeted condition with sufficient reasoning/output budget, not an invisible retry after seeing results.
4. The existing Gemma greedy/nonthinking protocol may stay frozen, while Qwen follows its documented instruction recipe. Label this an operational-system comparison: family, checkpoint adaptation and decoding differ, so a win is not a clean causal family effect. Within each system, plain versus assisted isolates the evidence intervention more directly.
5. Blind paired judgments of meaning, negation, agent/patient relations, omissions and unsupported additions; keep the evaluator provisional unless a qualified expert adjudicates. Report counts, item-level paired changes, truncation and uncertainty.24 reused DEV items support diagnosis and next-step selection, not a precise population accuracy estimate or untouched final evaluation.
