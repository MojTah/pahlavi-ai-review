## Future use and evidence contract

Use `training_admission.submit(api, spec, preparation, evidence_root)` for an authorized maintained training launch. `api` is supplied by the execution owner through a separately authorized existing account workflow; this helper does not authenticate. Do not call `api.run_job` directly with a draft or extracted intended command. `admit` supports offline inspection of an admitted specification, not a separate launch route.

The authoritative schema is `training_admission.validate`. In a single evidence root, `training-admission.json` binds six records by relative path, byte size and SHA-256:

| Record | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Contract | Exact job/settings/provenance, execution owner, nine nonempty artifact categories and frozen acquisition packet/count |
| Acquisition | Completed schedule with the same packet/count and hashed results |
| Decision | Reasoned justification for training bound to that contract and acquisition |
| Validation | Passing checks and their exact evidence bound to the contract |
| Review | Distinct `gpt-6-astra` reviewer approves the exact contract, acquisition, decision and validation |
| Authorization | Human authorizes the exact reviewed training job and execution owner |

Before a future authorized submission, stage the same evidence tree in the existing read-only input volume at `/input/training-admission/<job_sha256>`. Remote staging and readback have not been performed here. Any changed contract requires new validation/review/authorization. Keep the evidence root shared by the sole execution owner; its claim is not a provider-wide lock across copied roots. After an unknown result, inspect the exact provider job before manual recovery; never delete a claim to retry automatically.

These manual records prove byte bindings and declared status, not genuine reviewer identity, linguistic correctness or result-file completeness. The operator and Astra reviewer must inspect the contents and actual schedule. The mechanism cannot prevent deliberate code bypass or fabricated records. No synthetic test approval is a real approval.
